Modern care is fragmented. Patients move between specialists who don't talk to each other, receive disconnected guidance, and lose the sense of continuity. The **AGIR Method** organizes care across four interdependent pillars — Nutrition and Physical Activity, Metabolic Management, Body and Mind Integration, and Circadian Rhythm — and treats them as a single system over time.

Each pillar in isolation helps. The difference lies in **coordinating all four** with data, proximity and shared responsibility.
