# Capa — Prompt de Geração AI (apenas imagem)

Prompt para gerar **somente o fundo fotográfico** — sem texto. A tipografia (título, subtítulo, autor) é sobreposta depois em Figma/Canva com fontes reais e acentuação portuguesa correta.

**Gerador:** ChatGPT Plus (GPT Image 1.5) — fotorrealismo funciona bem nele.
**Alternativa:** foto real de Unsplash/Pexels com processamento no Figma (pode ser ainda melhor).

**Livro (PT):** ANTES · Dr. Getúlio Amaral Filho
**Subtítulo (PT):** A janela silenciosa entre o normal e o ótimo — onde a saúde é decidida.

**Livro (EN):** BEFORE · Dr. Getúlio Amaral Filho
**Subtítulo (EN):** The silent window between normal and optimal — where health is decided.

---

## Conceito

**Parede terracotta fotográfica.** Uma parede real — rebocada à mão, pigmento natural, patina de décadas de sol, sutilmente desbotada em alguns pontos, mais intensa em outros. Fotografia editorial close o suficiente pra mostrar textura e nuance, larga o suficiente pra suportar tipografia. Referências: Pompeia, Umbria, Alentejo português, taipa do sertão brasileiro.

A cor viva (terracotta) **pulsa vida** sozinha. A textura real **ancora em realismo**, premium, tátil — oposto de cor digital flat. A patina implica **tempo, permanência, calor de muitos verões** — coerente com o tema longevidade.

## Prompt

```
A high-resolution editorial photograph of a sun-warmed terracotta
wall. Vertical 2:3 aspect ratio, 1600×2560 px, print-ready.
Absolutely no text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no graffiti,
no posters, no signs, no markings of any kind.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
THE WALL — subject
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A close-up frontal view of a traditional hand-plastered wall in
natural terracotta pigment. The wall fills the ENTIRE frame — no
corners of other walls visible, no windows, no doors, no floor
line, no ceiling line, no architectural features. Pure wall
surface, edge-to-edge.

Surface character: hand-applied lime plaster with natural iron-
oxide/terracotta pigment mixed in. The surface is matte, not
glossy. Natural imperfections from the plastering process are
visible at close range — subtle ridges, tiny trowel marks, slight
undulation. The wall is WEATHERED but NOT RUINED — traces of decades
of sun and seasons have softened the surface without breaking it.

Color and nuance:
- Dominant tone: warm terracotta / burnt sienna #B84A2A range
- Natural variation across the wall — no uniform digital flatness
- Slightly lighter patches #D9683A in areas that receive more sun
- Slightly deeper tones #8E3820 in subtle shadows or lower areas
- Gentle tonal drift organic across the surface, like real pigment
- One or two very faint warm stains or watermarks — almost invisible
  unless looked for — that add authenticity

Subtle aging, NO dramatic damage:
- No large cracks, no chunks missing, no exposed bricks
- Maybe one hairline crack somewhere — restrained, not central
- Maybe soft areas where the color has faded slightly from the sun
- Maybe a whisper of salt weathering or water marking at the base
- The wall feels LOVED and USED, not abandoned or ruined

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
LIGHT
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Warm natural sunlight, mid-afternoon quality (color temperature
~5200K), falling across the wall at a slight oblique angle from
the upper left. The light is EVEN enough that the wall remains
readable across the whole surface, but has enough subtle gradient
(slightly brighter top-left, slightly darker bottom-right) to feel
natural and painterly, not flat digital.

No harsh shadows. No dramatic contrast. No shafts of light from
unseen objects (no window shadow patterns, no tree-leaf patterns,
no shutter patterns). Just clean natural sun on a continuous
surface.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
PHOTOGRAPHY STYLE
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

Medium-format editorial photography. Sharp focus across the entire
wall — no selective depth of field, no blur. Feels like a
deliberate, composed photograph taken for an architecture or
design magazine — Architectural Digest, World of Interiors, Cereal
Magazine, Apartamento.

Reference photographers: Luigi Ghirri (Italian walls in natural
light), Franco Fontana (Mediterranean color), Joel Meyerowitz
(Tuscany color), Stephen Shore (restrained editorial detail).

The photograph must read as REAL — taken by a human photographer
with a real camera, of a real wall that exists somewhere in a
sunlit Mediterranean village. Not CGI. Not AI-obvious.

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
REFERENCES — types of walls to look like
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Pompeii frescoes — Pompeian red pigment on plaster
- Umbrian farmhouse walls in late afternoon
- Alentejo (Portuguese) cottage walls
- Positano / Amalfi coast exterior walls
- Sertão (Brazilian interior) taipa walls
- Tuscan granary walls
- Moroccan tadelakt walls in warm pigment

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
HARD REJECTIONS — must not appear anywhere
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

- Text, letters, numbers, graffiti, writing, painted signs,
  murals, drawings, symbols, watermarks, logos of any kind
- Corners of other walls, ceiling lines, floor lines, baseboards,
  tile work, moldings, trim
- Windows, doors, openings, niches, alcoves, picture hooks,
  wall-mounted objects
- Plants, flowers, vines, ivy, leaves growing on or near wall
- Cast shadows from off-frame objects (window mullions, leaves,
  people, furniture)
- Dramatic cracks, large chunks missing, exposed brick, structural
  damage, ruin
- Harsh lighting, dramatic chiaroscuro, moody darkness
- Cold tones, grey tones, pink tones, orange-saturated tones
- Flat digital color, vector gradient, painterly illustration —
  this must read as a REAL PHOTOGRAPH of a REAL WALL
- Hyperreal HDR, over-sharpened, over-saturated — keep it
  natural editorial
- Posters, stickers, tape, nails, hooks, mounting hardware
- AI giveaways: impossible angles, repeating patterns in the
  texture, symmetric artifacts, overly smooth areas, plastic
  feel

──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
QUALITY DIRECTIVES
──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

The final image must accommodate typography being overlaid on top
of the wall in ivory serif (#F0E9D9) — so the wall must have
enough tonal depth to contrast with light text. The wall should
feel TACTILE — the viewer should want to reach out and touch it.
Natural. Warm. Alive. Real.

Style: editorial architectural photography, Mediterranean terracotta
wall, natural sunlight, weathered pigment patina, tactile surface.

--ar 2:3 --style raw --v 7 --s 30 --q 2
```

## Alternativa realista: foto real do Unsplash

Antes de gerar no AI, **vale tentar buscar uma foto real**. Unsplash tem excelentes fotos de paredes terracotta grátis:

**Buscar em** https://unsplash.com/:
- "terracotta wall"
- "pompeii red wall"
- "sienna plaster wall"
- "mediterranean wall texture"

Se achar uma boa, usa direto — qualidade fotográfica real sempre vence o AI para texturas. O AI tende a suavizar demais, criar padrões repetitivos, perder a imperfeição genuína.

## Pós-produção — tipografia overlay

Após ter a imagem (AI ou Unsplash):

1. Importa em **Figma** (grátis) ou **Canva Pro**
2. Ajuste se necessário: levemente escurecer o quadrante inferior para contraste com o rodapé (author line) — gradiente sutil
3. Sobrepõe tipografia:
   - **ANTES** em serif clássico (GT Sectra Bold, Canela Text Bold, Cormorant Garamond Bold) em ivory `#F0E9D9`, centralizado, **terço superior**, escala ~50% da largura
   - **Subtítulo** *"Do normal ao ótimo: longevidade, performance e prevenção"* em italic serif menor, ivory, abaixo do título, centralizado em 3 linhas balanceadas
   - **DR. GETÚLIO AMARAL FILHO** em small caps sans-serif (Inter Medium), letter-spacing 0.25em, ivory, rodapé
4. Export 2560×1600 JPG sRGB para KDP
