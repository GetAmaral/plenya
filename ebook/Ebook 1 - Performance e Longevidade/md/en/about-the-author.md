# About the Author

![Getulio Amaral Filho](images/autor.jpg)

**Dr. Getulio Amaral Filho** is a nephrologist and internal medicine physician with more than 20 years of clinical practice. He earned his medical degree from the State University of Londrina (Universidade Estadual de Londrina, UEL) in 2004, completed an internal medicine residency in 2006, and a nephrology fellowship in 2008, both at Santa Casa de Londrina, where he now coordinates the Nephrology Residency program and founded the Internal Medicine Residency. He serves as Medical Director of the DaVita in-hospital dialysis unit in Londrina and, in 2026, completed graduate training in Integrative Functional Medicine through the Brazilian Association of Integrative Functional Medicine (ABMFI).

He lectures nationally on health, nephrology, and longevity, translating complex science into accessible language for both lay and medical audiences. Over two decades he has cared for thousands of patients — and it was from that practice that the ACTS Method was born, along with the conviction that health is decided in the silent window between *normal* and *optimal*. From that same practice, this book was born.
