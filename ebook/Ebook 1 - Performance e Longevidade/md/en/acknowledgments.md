# Acknowledgments

This book was written in the gaps between consultations, hospital visits, meetings, and lectures — and, above all, in the spaces other people opened so that it could exist.

I thank my patients. Those who stayed, those who left too soon, and those who keep arriving at the office with the question that drives this book: *"Doctor, how is this possible?"* You are the reason for the work and the permanent test of my practice.

To my wife, **Fernanda**, who reads my silences and brings me back to the present when the office takes me too far. To my children, **Lucas** and **Bianca** — you are why I insist on aging well.

To my father, **Getulio** — father and mentor, the first physician I ever knew, on whose shoulders I keep learning. To my mother, **Ana**, the source of inspiration and of the integrative gaze that runs through this book. To my sister **Ana**, who showed me the path of integrative functional medicine, prevention, and longevity, and to **Tatiana**, constant presences who would not fit into any acknowledgment. To **Danilo**, brother-in-law and longtime brother who has always walked beside me.

To the teams at **Plenya**, **Nefroclínica**, and **DaVita**. Physician friends, nurses, psychologists, nutritionists, and exercise specialists, where the ideas in this book stopped being concept and became clinical practice.

To the team that cared for the production of this volume — editors, copyeditors, designers, and the translation team that brought this book into English.

And finally, to the reader: thank you for opening this book. The work begins here, and it is yours.
