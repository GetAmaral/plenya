## 1. HERO PRINCIPAL (Home)

**Uso:** Background fullscreen da home page. Texto branco/cream sobreposto à esquerda. Precisa de área escura/neutra no lado esquerdo para legibilidade.

**Dimensão:** 1920 x 1280 px (landscape 3:2)

**Prompt:**

```
Golden hour sunlight filtering through tree branches and leaves in a lush forest clearing. Warm amber and honey-toned light rays streaming diagonally from the upper right, creating soft bokeh circles and natural lens flare. The left side of the image is slightly darker and shadowed (for text overlay legibility), while the right side glows with warm golden light penetrating through green and amber foliage. Soft mist or atmospheric haze between the trees adds depth. Forest floor has a natural dirt path. Color temperature very warm — dominant tones are deep amber (#A08456), muted forest green, warm brown bark, and soft golden highlights. No people. No buildings. No artificial elements. Photographic style: editorial nature photography, shallow depth of field on the foreground leaves, sharp mid-ground trees, dreamy atmospheric background. Shot on medium format camera. Cinematic color grading with lifted shadows and warm midtones. Aspect ratio 3:2 landscape.
```

## Notas para geração

### Configurações recomendadas

| Ferramenta | Configuração |
|---|---|
| DALL-E 3 | Natural style (não vivid). Landscape ou custom size. |
| Midjourney | `--ar 3:2` ou `--ar 4:3`. `--style raw`. `--s 200` para menos estilização. |
| Leonardo AI | PhotoReal v2. Cinematic preset. Negative prompt: "neon, gym, hospital, cold blue light, stock photo, clip art". |
| Ideogram | Realistic mode. Aspect ratio manual. |

### Diretrizes gerais

- **Tom:** Quente, dourado, natural. Nunca frio, azulado, clínico.
- **Pessoas:** Quando presentes, vistas de costas ou em silhueta. Nunca olhando para câmera. Roupa neutra (sem logos, sem neon).
- **Ambiente:** Natural ou arquitetônico premium. Nunca hospital, academia, escritório genérico.
- **Cores dominantes:** Âmbar/dourado (#A08456), bege/cream (#E6E1D8), petrol (#0E3B4F) como acento. Verde suave como suporte.
- **Estilo fotográfico:** Editorial, levemente desaturado, grain sutil, profundidade de campo rasa no fundo.

## 9. HERO — PÁGINA MÉTODO AGIR (Nova prioridade alta)

**Uso:** Background do hero da página `/metodo-agir`. Precisa evocar os 4 pilares (alimentação/atividade, gestão metabólica, corpo-mente, ritmo circadiano) **sem ser diagrama**. Deve ser editorial, atmosférica, simbólica.

**Dimensão:** 1920 x 1080 px (landscape 16:9)

### Opção A — Amanhecer em movimento (mais direto)

```
Wide cinematic landscape at golden hour dawn. A lone figure seen from a great distance, walking on a minimalist paved path that curves through a vast open landscape — perhaps a dew-covered field or a modernist architectural garden with clean geometric stone paths. The figure is small, almost abstract, moving from shadow toward warm light. Behind the figure, the sun is breaking through a soft morning mist, casting long golden rays across the scene. The color palette is dominated by warm amber and honey tones (#A08456) in the sunlight and sky, with deeper petrol blue (#0E3B4F) shadows in the foreground. The overall composition visually evokes four simultaneous ideas: movement (the walking figure = atividade física), rhythm (the dawn = ritmo circadiano), integration (body traversing landscape = corpo-mente), and discipline (the structured path = gestão/método). No visible gym equipment, no meditation poses, no yoga mats, no medical imagery. Just presence, rhythm, and light. Photography style: editorial fine art, large format camera, shallow atmospheric depth, warm film-like color grading, slight grain, quiet composition. Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.
```

### Opção B — Still life editorial (mais sutil)

```
Overhead fine art still life photograph on a natural linen or raw wood surface in warm morning light. Four understated elements arranged asymmetrically with generous negative space: (1) a ceramic bowl holding fresh figs, a few almonds and a sprig of rosemary — representing nutrition and physical vitality; (2) an open leather-bound clinician's notebook with a fountain pen resting across it, a barely visible handwritten line of numbers — representing metabolic data and measurement; (3) a single smooth river stone next to a small clear glass vessel holding water — representing mind-body integration and contemplation; (4) a vintage brass pocket watch laid face-up beside a dried grass stem catching a single ray of light — representing circadian rhythm and time. All elements tied together by warm golden sunlight streaming at a low angle from the left, casting long shadows. Color palette strictly warm: amber (#A08456), cream (#E6E1D8), muted sage (#C2C9B1), and a single accent of deep petrol blue (#0E3B4F) in the leather notebook cover. No text. No logos. No obvious symbolism. The scene feels like it could be from a Kinfolk magazine or a Peter Attia cabinet. Photography style: editorial still life, overhead top-down composition, warm natural window light only, shallow depth of field, subtle film grain, muted color grading. Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.
```

### Opção C — Arquitetônico com figura humana (mais institucional)

```
Interior architectural photograph of a minimalist modernist clinic space at golden hour. A large window or glass wall dominates the right side, filtering warm amber light into the room. Through the window, soft natural landscape is visible — trees, foliage. In the foreground, a single person seen from behind in soft silhouette, wearing warm neutral clothing (cream or camel tones), looking outward toward the light — not meditating, not posing, just standing in quiet presence. The interior materials are premium but restrained: smooth warm concrete or limestone walls (#E6E1D8), polished oak floor, a single vertical element of dark petrol blue (#0E3B4F) — perhaps a slim accent wall or a piece of furniture — that anchors the composition. Warm diagonal light rays cross the floor. The scene reads as "a space where healing happens by design, not by accident." No medical equipment. No gym. No branding. Photography style: architectural editorial, wide-angle but not distorted, warm natural light only, shallow subtle depth. Aspect ratio 16:9 landscape.
```

### Recomendação

**Opção A** (amanhecer em movimento) é a mais forte para o hero — simbologia clara sem ser literal, energia emocional, e atende ao claim "linha contínua" da marca.

Use na página: coloque como `bg-image` do hero do `/metodo-agir` com overlay `from-petrol/70 via-petrol/60 to-petrol`, o texto cream fica perfeitamente legível no lado esquerdo.

### Pós-processamento sugerido

1. Ajustar temperatura de cor para +10-15% warm
2. Levantar sombras levemente (lifted shadows = look premium)
3. Reduzir saturação geral em 5-10%
4. Aplicar grain sutil (0.5-1%)
5. Exportar como JPEG qualidade 85%, sRGB
6. Nomear: `hero.jpg`, `lifestyle-movement.jpg`, `lifestyle-nature.jpg`, `lifestyle-wellness.jpg`, `clinic-exterior.jpg`, `clinic-interior.jpg`, `hero-about.jpg`, `hero-score.jpg`
