## About (Summary)

```
Twenty years across inpatient wards, hemodialysis, ICU, and outpatient clinic gave me what preventive medicine most needs and rarely has: the final stage of the problem. I have seen how the patient ends up when ferritin, ApoB, coronary calcium score, fragmented sleep, and silent sarcopenia are ignored.

I am a nephrologist and internist, graduated in Medicine from UEL (Universidade Estadual de Londrina) in 2004, with residency in Internal Medicine and Nephrology at Santa Casa de Londrina, and Board Certification in Nephrology from the Brazilian Society of Nephrology (SBN, 2008). Since 2009 I have coordinated the Nephrology Medical Residency at Santa Casa, founded the institution's Internal Medicine Residency program, served as Professor of Clinical Semiology at PUC Paraná Londrina (2013–2014), have practiced at Nefroclínica Londrina since 2009, and serve as Medical Director at DaVita In-Hospital Londrina since 2021. In April 2026 I completed postgraduate training in Integrative Functional Medicine at ABMFI (Brazilian Academy of Integrative Functional Medicine).

Today I lead the clinical direction of Plenya in Londrina — a multidisciplinary team working on biomarker panels, imaging-based body composition, hormonal profiling, inflammatory markers, and longitudinal interpretation of each patient. We apply what two decades of practice taught me, intervening earlier than established disease would otherwise allow.

I am a member of the Cardiorenal Committee of the Brazilian Society of Nephrology (SBN).

Where I write, speak, and teach:
→ "Antes" (2026) — book on evidence-based performance and longevity (Brazilian Portuguese; English edition forthcoming)
→ Plenya · plenyasaude.com.br — Health, Performance & Longevity · Londrina, Brazil

→ Talks: The ACTS Method · The Four Silent Killers · The Silent Window

For press, speaking engagements, and institutional partnerships: contato@drgetulioamaralfilho.com.br
```
